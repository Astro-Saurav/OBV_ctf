# Code of Conduct — Operation Black Vault CTF

## Our Pledge

We, as organizers, contributors, and participants of **Operation Black Vault (OBV) CTF**, pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### ✅ Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other participants
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community
- Helping newcomers learn and grow in cybersecurity

### ❌ Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sharing flags, solutions, or hints during an **active live competition**
- Attacking the CTF infrastructure, platform (CTFd), or other teams' systems
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without explicit permission
- DDoS attacks on challenge servers or the CTFd platform
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Competition-Specific Rules

> These rules apply specifically during **live CTF events**:

1. **No flag sharing** — Do not share flags, exploits, or solutions with other teams during an active competition.
2. **No brute-forcing** — Do not attempt to brute-force the CTFd login or challenge flag submission endpoints.
3. **No attacking infrastructure** — Only attack challenge instances provided to you. Do not attack the CTFd platform itself or other participants' machines.
4. **Play fair** — Each team should solve challenges independently.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders and event organizers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces including the GitHub repository, Discord server (if applicable), and during live CTF events.

---

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the event organizers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Organizers are obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

| Violation | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| Inappropriate language or minor infraction | Private warning |
| Repeated violations or harassment | Temporary ban from community/event |
| Serious violations (doxxing, cheating, infrastructure attacks) | Permanent ban and disqualification |
